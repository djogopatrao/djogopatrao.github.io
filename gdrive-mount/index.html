Personal Drive Mount

A personal-use tool that mounts my own Google Drive as a folder on my Linux computer.

What it does

This application is the OAuth client used by open-source tools (google-drive-ocamlfuse and rclone) running on the developer's personal computer. It lets the owner of a Google account browse, open, edit and back up their own Google Drive files through the local file system.

Who it is for

It is used only by its developer, for personal purposes. It is not a commercial product, is not distributed, and does not offer accounts or services to the public.

Google data

The app requests access to Google Drive only to show and sync the signed-in user's own files on their own computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
