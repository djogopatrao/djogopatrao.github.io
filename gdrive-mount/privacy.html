Privacy Policy

Personal Drive Mount · Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Data accessed

When you sign in with Google and grant consent, the app requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) to list, read, create, modify and delete files and folders in your own Google Drive. It also receives your basic account identifier as part of the standard Google sign-in.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to show your Drive as a local folder on your own computer and to keep local copies in sync with Google Drive. The app does not analyze, profile, or use your data for advertising, and it does not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model.

3. Storage

All processing happens locally on the computer where the tool is installed. OAuth tokens and cached file contents are stored only on that computer (for example, in ~/.gdfuse or the rclone configuration file). The developer runs no server and receives no copy of your data.

4. Sharing

Your Google data is not sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party. It goes only between your computer and Google's servers.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete local data, unmount the folder and remove the local configuration and cache directories. Your files stay in your Google Drive.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions: open an issue or contact the developer through github.com/djogopatrao.

Resumo em português

O app pede acesso ao Google Drive apenas para mostrar e sincronizar os arquivos do próprio usuário no computador dele. Tokens e cache ficam somente nesse computador; o desenvolvedor não mantém servidores e não recebe dados. Nada é vendido nem compartilhado com terceiros, e o uso segue a Política de Dados do Usuário dos Serviços de API do Google, incluindo os requisitos de Uso Limitado. O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento em myaccount.google.com/permissions.
